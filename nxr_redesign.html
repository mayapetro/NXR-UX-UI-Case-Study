NXR redesign

A cleaner, working version of the rental app's entry flow. Every field validates, buttons respond, and the flow connects end to end. Jump to a screen or just start tapping.

What changed and why

  • One ink colour, one accent. Deep navy on white, with pink saved for hearts, rewards and the confirmation check.
  • Lines, not boxes. Form fields are underlined and sections are separated by hairlines instead of cards and shadows.
  • No banner bars. A small NXR wordmark replaces the heavy navy header and wave.
  • One strong action per screen. A single solid button, with everything else quiet.
  • Photos carry the colour. The interface stays neutral so the vehicles stand out.
  • Ride-day screens. Tap START to begin a rental, watch a live ride timer, return the vehicle and rate the trip.
  • Help in one place. Support, a directions map with arrival time, and tidy FAQ, tutorials and tips lists.
  • Sunny gold background. Screens glow in warm gold with navy buttons, and gold only appears in small details like the active tab, check marks and rewards.
  • Slides 21 to 41 built in. Search with keyboard, date ranges, number boxes, the three-state map, and every ride-day screen follow your slides in this gold design.
